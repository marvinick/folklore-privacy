Privacy Policy

for ScriptureIQ

Last Updated: November 29, 2025

Introduction

ScriptureIQ ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our ScriptureIQ mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Quiz Responses: Your answers to quiz questions are stored locally on your device
  • Progress Data: Quiz scores, streaks, and question history are stored locally
  • Settings Preferences: Category selections and app preferences are stored locally

Information Automatically Collected

  • App Usage: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve app performance
  • Device Information: Basic device and operating system information for compatibility purposes
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs and improve stability

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the quiz functionality
  • Track your personal progress and statistics
  • Improve the app experience and add new features
  • Fix technical issues and bugs
  • Ensure app security and prevent misuse

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All personal quiz data (scores, history, preferences) is stored locally on your device
  • This data is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly opt-in to cloud backup features
  • You can delete all stored data through the app's settings menu

Security Measures

  • We implement appropriate technical safeguards to protect your information
  • All network communications use standard encryption protocols
  • We do not store personal information on our servers without your explicit consent

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights or the safety of users

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (optional, can be disabled)
  • Crash Reporting: Anonymous crash data to improve app stability
  • Question Content: Biblical questions are sourced from public domain or appropriately licensed content

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Review what data the app stores about you
  • Delete: Remove all stored quiz data through the app settings
  • Opt-Out: Disable optional analytics and data collection
  • Portability: Export your quiz statistics if available in future updates

Children's Privacy

This app is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app's quiz functionality works entirely with locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Providing in-app notification of significant changes

Data Retention

  • Local Data: Remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous analytics are retained for up to 2 years for improvement purposes
  • Crash Reports: Retained for up to 1 year to maintain app stability

International Users

This app is designed for global use. If you are located outside of the United States, please be aware that information you provide may be transferred to and processed in countries with different privacy laws.
