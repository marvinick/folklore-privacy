Privacy Policy

for FartOracle

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

Introduction

FartOracle ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our FartOracle mobile application (the "App"), a bio-acoustic analysis and wellness tracking tool.

Information We Collect

Acoustic Analysis Data

  • Real-Time Waveforms: The App processes acoustic signals via the device microphone in real-time. This audio data is processed 100% on-device and is NEVER saved to permanent storage or transmitted to our servers.
  • Neural Network Inference: Sound analysis is performed locally using Apple's SoundAnalysis framework.

Information Stored Locally

  • Analysis History: Your past analysis results, including timestamps, confidence levels, and identified triggers, are stored locally on your device.
  • Oracle Journal: Any dietary notes or personal annotations you add to records are stored locally.
  • App Settings: Your persona selection (Clinical vs. Sarcastic) and haptic preferences are stored locally.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide real-time bio-acoustic analysis and dietary predictions.
  • Enable long-term tracking of digestive patterns in the "Digestive Tracker."
  • Personalize the user experience based on the selected "Oracle Persona."
  • Maintain app security and prevent data loss (via local backups only).

Data Storage and Security

100% Local Storage

  • All sensitive data (history, journal notes) is stored within the App's private sandbox on your device.
  • This data is NOT transmitted to any cloud service or external server.
  • You can purge all stored data instantly at any time through "The Vault" settings menu.

Security Measures

  • We utilize Apple's standard device encryption to protect local data.
  • We do not collect or store any identifying personal information (names, emails, addresses) within the regular app workflow.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Every analysis and every note stays on your device.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Review all historical records within the App.
  • Delete: Purge your entire history and all journal notes via the Settings.
  • Permission Control: Revoke microphone access at any time through iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

FartOracle is designed to be a family-friendly wellness and entertainment tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. All data processing occurs locally on the user's device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the new policy within the App.

Data Retention

  • Local Data: Remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
